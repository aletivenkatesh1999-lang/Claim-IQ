Health Insurance Policy Guidelines

Policy Overview

This health insurance policy provides hospitalization coverage for
eligible insured members, subject to the policy terms, coverage limit,
waiting periods, exclusions, and claim conditions.

Hospitalization Coverage

Hospitalization expenses may be covered when the insured member is
admitted to a recognized hospital for medically necessary treatment.

Eligible expenses may include:

1. Hospital room charges within the applicable room-rent limit.
2. Nursing expenses.
3. Doctor and specialist consultation charges.
4. Operation theatre charges.
5. Prescribed medicines used during hospitalization.
6. Diagnostic and laboratory tests connected to the hospitalization.
7. Eligible intensive care unit charges.
8. Ambulance expenses within the applicable policy limit.

Claim Notification

For planned hospitalization, the insured member should inform the
insurer or third-party administrator before admission whenever
pre-authorization is required.

For emergency hospitalization, the insured member or representative
should notify the insurer or third-party administrator as soon as
reasonably possible after admission.

Required Claim Documents

The claimant must submit the applicable documents:

1. Completed and signed health insurance claim form.
2. Health insurance policy or health-card details.
3. Hospital admission and discharge summary.
4. Final hospital bill.
5. Detailed itemized hospital invoice.
6. Payment receipts.
7. Doctor prescriptions.
8. Diagnostic and laboratory reports.
9. Pharmacy bills supported by valid prescriptions.
10. Identification and bank-account details where required.
11. Accident-related police report where applicable.
12. Any additional medical records requested during claim review.

Cashless Claims

For a cashless claim, treatment should normally be taken at an eligible
network hospital.

The network hospital submits the pre-authorization request to the
insurer or third-party administrator.

Cashless approval is subject to policy eligibility, available coverage,
medical necessity, exclusions, waiting periods, and receipt of required
medical information.

Cashless authorization does not guarantee final settlement of every
hospital expense. Non-payable expenses may remain the responsibility
of the insured member.

Reimbursement Claims

For reimbursement claims, the insured member may pay the hospital
directly and subsequently submit the claim documents to the insurer.

The submitted claim is reviewed against the policy coverage, available
sum insured, sub-limits, exclusions, waiting periods, and medical
necessity.

Waiting Period

Certain medical conditions or treatments may be subject to a waiting
period.

Claims arising during an applicable waiting period may not be payable
unless the policy explicitly provides coverage.

The exact waiting period depends on the approved policy schedule and
policy wording.

Pre-Existing Conditions

Pre-existing conditions may be covered only after completion of the
waiting period stated in the applicable policy schedule.

The insured member must provide accurate medical information during
policy enrollment and claim processing.

Material non-disclosure may affect claim eligibility.

Common Exclusions

The following may be excluded unless the applicable policy explicitly
provides coverage:

1. Treatment during an applicable waiting period.
2. Cosmetic treatment that is not medically necessary.
3. Experimental or unproven treatment.
4. Treatment received only for diagnostic observation without covered
   hospitalization.
5. Treatment resulting from intentional self-inflicted injury.
6. Expenses unrelated to the covered hospitalization.
7. Non-medical and administrative items not covered by the policy.
8. Treatment obtained through fraudulent or materially misleading
   information.

Claim Assessment

The claims team verifies:

1. Whether the policy was active on the treatment date.
2. Whether the insured member was eligible.
3. Whether the treatment was medically necessary.
4. Whether the condition was covered.
5. Whether a waiting period applied.
6. Whether exclusions or sub-limits applied.
7. Whether the required documents were submitted.
8. Whether sufficient coverage remained under the policy.

Additional information may be requested from the claimant, hospital,
doctor, or third-party administrator before a final decision is made.

Claim Status Workflow

A newly created claim begins in Submitted status.

A valid claim may progress through:

Submitted
Under Review
Approved or Rejected
Closed

A claim should not move backward through the workflow unless an
authorized claims manager records an override and justification.

Fraud and Misrepresentation

The claim may be investigated when submitted information appears
incomplete, altered, inconsistent, duplicated, or intentionally
misleading.

Suspected fraud does not automatically establish that fraud occurred.
The claim must be reviewed according to the approved investigation and
decision process.

No-Match Rule for the AI Assistant

The AI assistant must answer only from approved active policy and
guideline documents.

If the approved documents do not contain the requested information, the
assistant must respond:

No relevant information was found in the approved policy documents.

The assistant must not invent coverage amounts, waiting periods,
eligibility conditions, benefits, exclusions, or claim decisions.

Every retrieved answer must include the source document and source
section used.
